# 1. Deep Dive 3D

**Category:** Science, Medical Explainers, and Human Body Education

**Visual Style:** Cinematic 3D Animation

### Overview

Deep Dive 3D is designed for educational videos that explain scientific processes, human biology, and interesting what-if scenarios.

It uses stylized 3D characters, realistic environments, glowing X-ray cutaways, and detailed views of internal body systems.

The visual presentation moves between three different environments:

1. Real World – A character in a warm, realistic environment.
2. X-Ray World – A transparent cyan body showing bones and organs.
3. Inside the Body – Close-up views of organs, blood vessels, and internal biological processes.

### Visual Characteristics

- Stylized 3D human characters.
- Warm, sunlit environments.
- Glowing cyan X-ray visuals.
- Deep red internal-body scenes.
- Cinematic camera movements.
- Shallow depth of field.
- Detailed lighting and shadows.
- Smooth transitions between environments.

### Recommended Topics

- What happens if you hold your breath?
- What happens if you swallow chewing gum?
- What happens inside your body when you get sick?
- How does the human heart work?
- What happens when an insect stings you?

### Animation Guidelines

- Keep the camera moving throughout each shot.
- Use push-ins, camera orbits, and slow camera drifts.
- Transition between real-world and internal-body scenes.
- Keep important visual elements clearly visible.
- Avoid graphic injuries and gore.

### Caption Style

- White captions.
- Dark outlines.
- One highlighted yellow word.
- Short captions containing approximately 1–3 words.
- Sentence-case typography.

### Technical Details

| Setting | Original Specification |
|---|---|
| Resolution | 1080 × 1920 |
| Frame Rate | 24 FPS |
| Video Duration | 30–45 seconds |
| Voice | Kokoro am_michael |
| Visual Engine | Three.js |
| Rendering | Google Chrome and FFmpeg |

# 2. Night File 3D

**Category:** Mysteries, True Crime, and Investigative Stories

**Visual Style:** Dark Cinematic 3D

### Overview

Night File 3D is designed for mysterious, suspenseful, and investigative storytelling.

It uses dark environments, isolated lighting, faceless characters, red highlights, and documentary-style evidence elements.

The atmosphere is intentionally quiet, dark, and unsettling.

### Visual Characteristics

- Dark 3D environments.
- Single-source lighting.
- Near-black faceless characters.
- Red glowing subjects.
- Thin red rim lighting.
- Film grain.
- Slow camera movements.
- Evidence boards and documentary-style inserts.

### Visual Elements

The style can use:

- Case files.
- Polaroid photographs.
- Cassette tapes.
- Red circles.
- Redacted documents.
- Date stamps.
- Evidence walls.
- Dark corridors.
- Night-time environments.

Red is reserved for the main subject and important evidence markings.

### Recommended Topics

- Unsolved mysteries.
- Historical disappearances.
- Strange real-world incidents.
- Famous aviation mysteries.
- Documented criminal investigations.
- Unexplained historical events.

### Storytelling Structure

A typical Night File story follows this structure:

1. Introduce the date and location.
2. Introduce the person involved.
3. Explain the unusual event.
4. Present the evidence step by step.
5. Reveal the critical incident.
6. Explain what investigators discovered.
7. End with the unresolved question.

### Animation Guidelines

- Use slow camera push-ins and drifts.
- Keep shots approximately 3–5 seconds.
- Use isolated lighting.
- Maintain a consistent dark atmosphere.
- Use evidence inserts to support narration.
- Use silence and sound effects to create suspense.

### Caption Style

- Typewriter-style typography.
- Cream-colored text.
- One red highlighted word.
- Approximately 2–5 words per caption.
- Letter-by-letter text animation.

### Technical Details

| Setting | Original Specification |
|---|---|
| Aspect Ratio | 9:16 |
| Voice | Kokoro am_onyx |
| Voice Speed | 0.95 |
| Visual Engine | Three.js-based 3D engine |
| Music | Tension |
| Caption Font | FreeMono |

### Content Safety

- Do not show graphic violence.
- Do not show weapons.
- Treat victims and their families respectfully.
- Verify real-world cases using reliable sources.
- Never accuse a real living person without established evidence.

THE MAN WHO JUMPED OUT OF A PLANE

# 3. Inside Out

**Category:** Science, Health, and Educational Explainers

**Visual Style:** Flat 2D Cartoon Animation

### Overview

Inside Out is designed for quick educational videos explaining scientific concepts, human biology, and interesting what-if scenarios.

It uses simple cartoon characters, clean vector shapes, X-ray cutaways, and colorful visual explanations.

Unlike Deep Dive 3D, this style uses flat 2D illustrations rather than realistic 3D environments.

### Visual Characteristics

- Flat 2D illustrations.
- Minimal cartoon characters.
- Simple geometric shapes.
- Clean backgrounds.
- Bright visual colors.
- X-ray-style body cutaways.
- Expressive facial reactions.
- Fast camera movements.

### Color Palette

The original style guide specifies:

| Color | Hex Code | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Skin | #E8B89A | Character skin |
| Organ Pink | #E57A8A | Internal organs |
| Danger Red | #D62839 | Warnings and danger |
| X-Ray Cyan | #4FD1E8 | Internal-body views |
| Highlight Yellow | #FFD43B | Important caption words |
| Background | #F4F1EA | Background |
| Ink | #1A1A1E | Outlines and details |

### Recommended Topics

- What happens if you swallow chewing gum?
- What happens if you eat too much sugar?
- How does digestion work?
- What happens when you get dehydrated?
- Interesting human body facts.

### Animation Guidelines

- Use one clear visual idea per shot.
- Change scenes approximately every 1–3 seconds.
- Use expressive facial reactions.
- Use X-ray views to explain internal processes.
- Use camera punch-ins and transitions.
- Keep visuals simple and understandable.

### Caption Style

- Bold white typography.
- Dark outlines and shadows.
- One yellow highlighted word.
- Approximately 1–3 words per caption.
- Centered mobile-friendly positioning.

# 4. Stick Story

**Category:** Survival Scenarios, Life Hacks, and Hypothetical Situations

**Visual Style:** 2D Stick-Figure Animation

### Overview

Stick Story is designed for entertaining educational videos involving survival situations, practical advice, and hypothetical scenarios.

It uses simple black stick figures with large expressive eyes, illustrated environments, and numbered step cards.

The storytelling approach is straightforward, making complicated situations easier to understand.

### Visual Characteristics

- Black stick-figure characters.
- Large expressive white eyes.
- Flat illustrated environments.
- Daytime and nighttime scenes.
- Gold numbered step cards.
- Red danger indicators.
- Green safety indicators.
- Simple animated objects.

### Recommended Topics

- What would you do if the electricity went out for three days?
- How would you survive without clean water?
- What should you do during an emergency?
- Essential survival tips.
- Everyday emergency scenarios.

### Animation Guidelines

- Use one main visual idea per narration clause.
- Change scenes approximately every 1.5–3 seconds.
- Make character eyes react before body movements.
- Keep the same environment throughout the story.
- Use darker environments for dangerous situations.
- Use brighter environments for positive conclusions.

### Visual Indicators

| Color | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Gold | Steps and instructions |
| Red | Danger and warnings |
| Green | Safety and correct actions |
| Black | Characters |

### Caption Style

- Sentence-case typography.
- Dark rounded caption bars.
- One gold highlighted word.
- Approximately 2–4 words per caption.
- Automatic text fitting.

### Technical Details

| Setting | Original Specification |
|---|---|
| Aspect Ratio | 9:16 |
| Visual Engine | JavaScript-based 2D animation |
| Character Style | Black stick figures |
| Voice | Kokoro am_puck |
| Music | Bed |

### Content Safety

For survival and emergency-related content:

- Verify safety recommendations.
- Use reliable official sources.
- Prefer government and recognized emergency organizations.
- Avoid presenting dangerous experiments as practical advice.

# 5. Paper Atlas

**Category:** History, Geography, and Historical Storytelling

**Visual Style:** Animated Paper Cut-Outs

### Overview

Paper Atlas is designed for historical storytelling and educational videos.

It creates the appearance of an animated history book using paper-cut illustrations, aged parchment, animated maps, historical figures, and timeline elements.

The style combines documentary storytelling with a storybook visual presentation.

### Visual Characteristics

- Aged parchment backgrounds.
- Paper-cut illustrations.
- Soft paper shadows.
- Animated maps.
- Crimson travel routes.
- Gold date ribbons.
- Wax seals.
- Historical characters.
- Torn-paper caption strips.

### Recommended Topics

- Ancient civilizations.
- Historic battles.
- Famous historical events.
- Ancient empires.
- Historical voyages.
- The rise and fall of kingdoms.
- Interesting historical disasters.

### Animation Guidelines

- Introduce the location first.
- Explain the historical context.
- Introduce the important people.
- Show events in chronological order.
- Use maps to explain locations and movements.
- Use timelines to organize historical events.
- End with the consequences and historical impact.

### Color System

| Color | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Crimson | Routes, important targets, and key words |
| Gold | Dates, crowns, and seals |
| Aged Paper | Main background |
| Navy | Night-time scenes |

### Caption Style

- Serif typography.
- Torn-paper caption strips.
- Approximately 2–4 words per caption.
- One crimson highlighted word.

### Technical Details

| Setting | Original Specification |
|---|---|
| Aspect Ratio | 9:16 |
| Visual Engine | JavaScript-based paper-cut animation |
| Main Font | Caladea |
| Voice | Kokoro bm_george |
| Music | Bed or Tension |

### Historical Accuracy

- Verify historical dates and numbers.
- Use museums, encyclopedias, and primary records.
- List research sources in the project script.
- Do not invent historical quotations.
- Avoid depicting graphic violence.

# 6. Tall Tale 3D

**Category:** Funny True Stories, Unusual Events, and Historical Stories

**Visual Style:** 2D Cartoon Characters Inside a 3D World

### Overview

Tall Tale 3D is designed for entertaining storytelling involving unusual real-world events, funny historical stories, strange loopholes, and unexpected situations.

Its defining visual concept is the combination of flat 2D cartoon characters with colorful 3D environments.

The characters are illustrated cut-outs that interact with fully developed three-dimensional surroundings.

### Visual Characteristics

- Expressive 2D cartoon characters.
- Bright 3D environments.
- Thick black outlines.
- Large round cartoon heads.
- Simple cartoon bodies.
- Colorful miniature environments.
- Moving cinematic cameras.
- Expressive character reactions.
- Detailed shadows and lighting.

### Recommended Topics

- Funny true stories.
- Strange historical incidents.
- Unusual business stories.
- Crazy loopholes.
- Famous heists.
- Unexpected historical events.
- Interesting real-world personalities.

### Animation Guidelines

- Keep characters in a consistent visual style.
- Use expressive facial reactions.
- Move the camera throughout every shot.
- Introduce a new shot approximately every 1.5–3 seconds.
- Use camera push-ins, pans, and crane movements.
- Make characters react to important narration moments.
- Use bright daylight for outdoor environments.
- Use warm lighting for indoor environments.

### Storytelling Structure

1. Start with the most surprising fact.
2. Rewind and explain how it happened.
3. Show the events step by step.
4. Use short character reaction moments.
5. Reveal the unexpected outcome.
6. Finish with a funny or surprising punchline.

### Caption Style

- Bold white typography.
- Thick black outlines.
- Soft shadows.
- One word at a time.
- Short words may be grouped together.
- No colored keyword highlighting.

# 8. General Video Quality Guidelines

Regardless of the selected animation style, follow these guidelines.

### Opening Hook

Capture attention within the first two seconds whenever appropriate.

Avoid unnecessary introductions.

### Visual Pacing

Keep the visuals moving and make scene changes match the narration.

### Consistent Characters

Maintain consistent character appearances throughout the video.

### Caption Accuracy

Ensure captions match the spoken narration.

### Audio Quality

Keep voiceovers clear and balance background music with sound effects.

### Visual Quality Control

Inspect the generated frames and final video before publishing.

Check for:

- Incorrect animations.
- Overlapping objects.
- Poor camera framing.
- Unreadable captions.
- Audio synchronization problems.
- Visual inconsistencies.

### Copyright and Research

- Use original scripts.
- Verify factual claims.
- Respect third-party copyrights.
- Check music, font, and asset licenses.
- Never redistribute original source files without permission.
